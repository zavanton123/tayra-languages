How to learn with Tayra Languages

Welcome to Tayra Languages! This short book shows you how to use the app to learn a language by reading. It is written in the language you are learning, so it is also your first practice text. Do not worry if you do not understand every word yet.

Why reading works

We learn a language best from many interesting texts that we can almost understand. When you know most of the words on a page, you can guess the rest, and every new word is easier to remember. Tayra helps you find such texts and remembers every word you meet.

Colours show what you know

Words you have not saved yet are blue: they are new to you. Click a word to see its translation in your native language, to look it up in a dictionary or to hear how it sounds. Then choose how well you know it. The statuses from 1 to 4 mean that you are still learning the word, and its colour gets lighter as you learn it. Known means that you know the word well, and then it has no colour at all. Ignore is for names, numbers and other words you do not need to learn. You can also save a phrase of several words together.

Finish every page

At the end of a page, mark the remaining words as known. Every blue word that is left on the page becomes known. This is how Tayra learns which words you already know, so be honest: if a word is still not clear, click it and give it a learning status first. Then the next page shows fewer blue words.

Listen and translate

Press the play button to hear the text read aloud, sentence by sentence. With auto-pause on, the reading stops after each sentence, so you can repeat it. If a sentence is too hard, you can translate it, but first try to understand it on your own.

Courses and your own texts

The Courses screen has ready-made courses: short and simple stories, from the easiest level, A1, to the hardest, C2. Each course teaches the next hundred of the most common words of the language. You can download the courses of your language in Settings, under Courses. In Books you can add your own texts as well: paste an article, import a file, or make a course of your own from texts you choose.

Your vocabulary level

In Vocabulary, the word frequency list shows the most common words of your language. If you already know some of the language, set your vocabulary level there, and Tayra marks the most common words as known for you.

Flashcards

Every word you are learning gets a flashcard. Each day, review the cards that are due in Flashcards; a few minutes are enough. Reading is still the main work, and flashcards help the new words stay with you.

Make it a habit

Read a little every day, even for ten minutes. Choose texts you enjoy, and read them more than once. Listen and read at the same time. Do not save every word: save the words you want to learn, and keep reading. You can see your progress in the statistics, and in the words that are no longer blue.

On a computer, keyboard shortcuts make reading faster. The numbers from 1 to 4 set a status, K marks a word as known, I ignores it, T translates the sentence and the space bar plays or pauses the audio. You will find all of them in Settings, under Keyboard shortcuts.

In Settings you can also choose your native language, the language of the interface, offline dictionaries and voices. Now open Courses, choose the first course and start reading. Good luck!
